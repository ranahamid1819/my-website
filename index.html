How it works

  1. 1
    Add your API key

    Open Tools, press API Keys and paste a free key from Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter. Two or more keys work best.

  2. 2
    Upload your files

    Open File Metadata, choose Adobe Stock, Shutterstock or Freepik, then add your JPG, PNG, MP4, EPS or AI files.

  3. 3

FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

What is Tagvio?

A free AI tool for stock contributors. It names your files and writes titles, keywords and categories for Adobe Stock, Shutterstock and Freepik.

How does it work?

Pick a platform, upload your files and press Generate Metadata. Check the result, edit anything you want, then download your files or a CSV.

Do I need to pay?

No, Tagvio is free. You only need an API key. Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter give free keys. OpenAI is paid.

Where do I get a free API key?

Get one from Google AI Studio (the easiest), Groq or OpenRouter. Then open Tools, press API Keys and paste it. You only do this once.

Which files can I upload?

JPG, PNG, MP4, EPS and AI, many at once. For EPS and AI, save the file with a preview in Illustrator.

Are my files safe?

Yes. Your files stay on your device. Only a small copy of each image is sent to the AI so it can see it. Your keys are saved only in your browser.

Why add more than one key?

More keys mean faster work and fewer stops from free limits. You can save up to 5, and Tagvio switches between them by itself.

What do the names look like?

4 to 8 simple words joined by hyphens, like rough-grey-concrete-grain-texture-background.

I do not like a name. What can I do?

Press New name to get a different one, or type your own.

Is Tagvio part of Adobe Stock, Shutterstock or Freepik?

No. Tagvio is an independent free tool made for contributors. Read our Privacy and Terms pages for the details.

Does it change my original files?

No. You download new copies with the new names. Your original files stay as they are.

Privacy

Short and clear: what happens to your files and data on Tagvio. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Your files

Tagvio runs in your browser. Your original files are never uploaded to a Tagvio server, because Tagvio has no server. Renaming, CSV export and writing metadata into JPG and PNG files all happen on your own device.

What goes to the AI service

To write metadata, a resized copy of each image (or three frames of a video) is sent directly from your browser to the AI service you choose: Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, xAI or OpenAI. That service handles it under its own privacy policy. Tagvio never sees these requests.

Your API keys and settings

Your API keys, limits and preferences are saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. You can delete them any time from API Keys, or by clearing your browser data.

Cookies and tracking

Tagvio uses no cookies, no ads and no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; both may see your IP address as part of delivering the page.
